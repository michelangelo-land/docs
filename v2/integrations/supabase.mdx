The Supabase integration lets you give any Michelangelo app its own real backend, powered by your own Supabase project.

## What Supabase Adds

Supabase provides a managed database, authentication, and storage. Connecting it to a Michelangelo project means your app can store data, manage users, and scale beyond the device.

## How It Works

Every Michelangelo app links to one dedicated Supabase project. A Supabase project already backing another app cannot be selected again.

You can connect in two places:

**From Home, before the app exists:**

1. Tap the `+` button in the prompt input.
2. Choose **Supabase**.
3. Tap **Add Supabase Account** and complete the sign-in in the browser.
4. Select one of your Supabase projects from the picker.
5. Describe your app, including its backend needs, and submit the prompt.

**From App Settings, for an existing app:**

1. Open a project in the editor.
2. Tap the settings icon in the header.
3. Go to **Supabase**.
4. Tap **Add Supabase Account** if you have not connected yet.
5. Tap **Select project** and choose one of your Supabase projects.

In both cases, describe backend needs in plain language, such as:

- "Save user profiles to a database."
- "Let users sign up and log in."
- "Show live updates when data changes."

Michelangelo generates the front-end code, creates the required tables in your Supabase project, and wires the app to it.

## What You Get

- A complete app connected to your own Supabase project.
- Database tables created for what you described.
- Sign-in-ready code using your project URL and anonymous key.
- Your data visible and manageable in the Supabase dashboard.

## Managing the Connection

Open **App Settings → Supabase** to see:

- **Connected Account** — the Supabase account you signed in with.
- **Project Database** — the Supabase project backing this app. Tap it to open the Supabase dashboard.
- **Actions** — change the linked project with **Change project**, or remove it with **Unlink project**.

Unlinking removes the link between the Michelangelo app and the Supabase project. It does not delete anything on Supabase.

## If You Have No Projects Yet

The picker helps you get set up without leaving the flow:

- Tap **Create project** or **Add new project** to open the Supabase dashboard and create one, then come back to pick it.
- Tap **Switch organization** to sign in again with a different Supabase organization.

<Info>
  The Supabase integration is available today in the Michelangelo iOS app. If your connection expires or is revoked, reconnect your account from the picker.
</Info>
