The **Usage** screen shows what Michelangelo made possible for you: how much you created, how consistently you build, and how much AI work went into your projects.

To open it:

1. Go to the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Usage**.

## Your Creative Power

The top section summarizes your total creative output:

- **Lines of code generated** — total lines across all your `CODE` files.
- **Code files created** — total number of `CODE` files across all your projects.
- **Current streak** — consecutive days with at least one app generated. The streak stays alive as long as your last activity was today or yesterday.
- **Best streak** — your longest run of consecutive active days.
- **Most productive month** — the month with the most apps generated, shown as `Mon YYYY · N apps` (for example, `Aug 2026 · 12 apps`).
- **Most active hour** — the hour of day when you create the most, shown in your device timezone (for example, `9 PM`).

Tap the info icon next to a metric to see its definition directly in the app.

## Contributions

The contributions calendar visualizes your activity day by day, GitHub-style:

- Each square is one day.
- The darker the square, the more apps you generated that day.
- The calendar covers a rolling window of the last 12 months.

<Info>
  Contributions are counted from **August 2026**.
</Info>

## This Month

Your app generation activity for a specific month:

- **Month / year selectors** — browse any month from the last 5 years.
- **Apps generated** — new projects created in the selected month.
- **AI generations** — every time the AI worked on one of your projects in the selected month, including edits to existing apps.
- **Tokens used** — total AI tokens for the selected month (input + output + cache read + cache creation), abbreviated as `K` / `M` for large values.

<Info>
  AI generations and tokens are counted from **August 2026**. If there is no data for the selected month, the app shows `—`.
</Info>

## All Time

Your total activity since you started:

- **Apps generated** — total projects created.
- **AI generations** — total AI runs across all your projects.
- **Tokens used** — total AI tokens across all time.

## How Counting Works

| Metric | What it counts |
| ------ | -------------- |
| Apps generated | New projects you created (`projects` table). Always available. |
| AI generations | Every AI run on your projects, including follow-up edits (`usage_events` table). Counting started in August 2026. |
| Tokens used | Sum of `input + output + cache read + cache creation` tokens from `usage_events`. Counting started in August 2026. |
| Streaks | Days with at least one new project or AI run. Computed in your device timezone. |
| Best month / peak hour | Aggregated from your project creation dates in your device timezone. |

The screen refreshes automatically when you create apps or when the AI finishes work, and again when the app returns to the foreground.
